Clinical Interpretation

About mDF Score

The modified Discriminant Function (mDF) is used to assess the severity of alcoholic hepatitis and help guide treatment decisions. An mDF score ≥32 suggests severe alcoholic hepatitis and may indicate the need for corticosteroid therapy.
